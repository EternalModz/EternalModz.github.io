Italian Food by Region

Italy has many regional food traditions. Local ingredients, climate, history, and family recipes have helped each region develop its own dishes and cooking styles.

Lazio

Lazio is the region that includes Rome. It is known for simple pasta dishes that use ingredients such as pecorino cheese, black pepper, eggs, and guanciale.

Sicily

Sicilian cooking is influenced by the island's Mediterranean location. Common ingredients include citrus, seafood, tomatoes, eggplant, pistachios, and ricotta.
