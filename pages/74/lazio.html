Food of Lazio

Lazio is a central Italian region and the home of Rome. Roman cooking is known for bold flavors made from a small number of ingredients.

Carbonara

Carbonara is one of Rome's best-known pasta dishes. Traditional versions are commonly made with pasta, eggs, pecorino romano, guanciale, and black pepper.

The eggs and cheese form a creamy sauce without using cream. The hot pasta helps bring the ingredients together.
