Sicilian Cuisine

Sicily is the largest island in the Mediterranean Sea. Its food reflects local agriculture, coastal life, and centuries of cultural influence.

Arancini

Arancini are breaded and fried rice balls with fillings such as meat sauce, cheese, or vegetables. They are a popular Sicilian street food.

Other Sicilian Foods

  • Pasta alla Norma with tomato, eggplant, and ricotta salata
  • Cannoli filled with sweet ricotta
  • Granita, a frozen dessert often served with brioche
